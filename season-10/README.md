# Season 10: Gloomsbury Manor

50 cards, plus a rules card and a card back. Select a card to open the full-size image.

- **Stats:** Eyes, Fingers, Toes
- **Balance:** built for Top Trumps. Every card's stats add up to 15, with at least one stat of 8 or more and one of 3 or less, so every card can win a round and every card can lose one. There are no rarities.
- **Data:** [cards.json](cards.json)
